Welcome to BRIANE (Barangay Rainfall Intelligence and Alert Notification for Emergencies). BRIANE is an SMS-based flood early warning system developed to monitor rainfall conditions, classify rainfall based on predefined mathematical thresholds, and provide alerts to registered users to support community preparedness.

By registering for or using the BRIANE System, you acknowledge and agree to the following Terms of Service.

Purpose of BRIANE

BRIANE is designed to provide rainfall-based information and SMS alerts that may help users become more aware of changing rainfall conditions and prepare for possible flooding.

The system is intended to support, not replace, official announcements, warnings, evacuation orders, and emergency instructions issued by the barangay, local government units, PAGASA, NDRRMC, or other authorized authorities.

Registration and Account Information

To use certain features of BRIANE, users may be required to register and provide information necessary for account creation and SMS notifications.

Users agree to:

  • Provide accurate and valid information during registration.
  • Provide an active mobile number capable of receiving SMS messages.
  • Keep their registration information updated when necessary.
  • Use only their own registered account.
  • Report incorrect account information or suspected unauthorized access to the system administrator or an authorized barangay representative.

Proper Use of the System

Users agree to use BRIANE only for its intended purposes and must not:

  • Provide false or misleading registration information.
  • Attempt to access restricted administrative features.
  • Interfere with, damage, or disrupt the operation of the system.
  • Attempt to gain unauthorized access to another user's account or information.
  • Use the system to distribute false, harmful, or unauthorized information.

Administrative functions and system configurations are restricted to authorized personnel.

Rainfall Alerts

BRIANE uses rainfall measurements and predefined rainfall thresholds to classify rainfall conditions and generate corresponding alerts.

The current rainfall classifications are:

Alert Level Rainfall Intensity
Awareness 7.5–15 mm/hr
Preparedness >15–30 mm/hr
Response >30 mm/hr

These classifications are intended to provide early information for awareness and preparedness. They do not guarantee that flooding will or will not occur.

Users should remain attentive to their surroundings and follow official warnings and instructions when issued.

SMS Notifications

BRIANE automatically sends SMS alerts when the system detects conditions that meet the programmed rainfall thresholds.

Successful and timely delivery of an SMS may depend on factors beyond the system's control, including mobile network availability, network congestion, device connectivity, power interruptions, and other technical conditions.

Therefore, BRIANE does not guarantee that every alert will be received immediately or successfully.

System Limitations

BRIANE is a research-based early warning system and may experience technical limitations, measurement errors, delays, interruptions, or equipment and communication failures.

Rainfall measurements alone may not account for all factors that contribute to flooding. Therefore:

  • The absence of a BRIANE alert does not mean that flooding will not occur.
  • Receiving a BRIANE alert does not necessarily mean that flooding will occur in a user's exact location.
  • BRIANE alerts should not be the sole basis for emergency or evacuation decisions.
  • Users should always consider official advisories and instructions from authorized authorities.

System Availability

The BRIANE Development Team will make reasonable efforts to maintain the system and its features. However, continuous or uninterrupted availability cannot be guaranteed.

The system may temporarily become unavailable because of maintenance, technical problems, power interruptions, sensor or hardware issues, network disruptions, or other circumstances beyond the team's control.

Research and Evaluation

BRIANE was developed as a research project to evaluate the functionality, accuracy, response time, and user-friendliness of an SMS-based flood early warning system using mathematical rainfall threshold models.

The system may undergo testing, evaluation, maintenance, and improvement as part of its research and development.

HOW TO REGISTER AND VERIFY YOUR BRIANE ACCOUNT

1. Sign In or Create an Account

If you already have an account, sign in by entering your registered email address and password. If you do not have an account, click “Create One Now” located at the lower-right portion of the sign-in page.

2. Enter Your Account Information

Provide the required personal information, including your email address and password. Make sure that the information you provide is accurate.

3. Create Your Account

Click “Create Account & Send Verification” to submit your registration and request an account verification email.

4. Check Your Email

Open your Gmail account and check your Spam folder if the verification email does not appear in your inbox. Look for an email from BRIANE.

5. Verify Your Email Address

Open the BRIANE verification email and click the verification link provided. A confirmation message will appear indicating that your account has been successfully verified.

6. Configure Your Mobile Number

Return to the BRIANE System website and enter your mobile number. Make sure to provide an active and accessible mobile number, as this will be used to receive BRIANE SMS alerts.

7. Start Using BRIANE

Your account is now officially registered and verified as a recipient and user of the BRIANE System. You may now explore the available features and view the rainfall data and other information provided through the system interface.
