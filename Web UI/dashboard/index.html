Flash Flood Safety & Go-Bag Essentials

Emergency "Go-Bag" Essentials

  • Drinking Water: 1 gallon per person per day (3-day supply).
  • Ready-to-Eat Food: Easy-open canned goods, energy biscuits.
  • Important Documents: IDs, titles, birth certs sealed in waterproof bag.
  • First Aid & Meds: Prescription medicines, antiseptic, bandages.
  • Power & Comms: Fully charged powerbank, whistle, waterproof flashlight.

Critical Flood Action Protocol

  • Evacuate Early: Do NOT wait until floodwaters reach your doorstep.
  • Electrical Safety: Switch off the main household electrical circuit breaker.
  • Never Walk or Drive in Floodwaters: Just 15cm of flowing water can knock an adult down; 30cm can float cars.
  • Stay Tuned: Keep your mobile phone active for official BRIANE SMS emergency alerts.

Install BRIANE on Your Phone

Detecting your device…

Web App (PWA)

BRIANE installs as a Progressive Web App — a real app icon on your home screen, without going through the app store.

What installing does

  • Adds a BRIANE icon to your phone's home screen, so you can open it like any other app.
  • Opens full-screen without the browser address bar and with only the dashboard to look at.
  • Keeps working offline — the last readings and alerts are cached on your device.
  • Flood alerts still arrive as push notifications even when the app is closed.
  • Always current — updates install themselves the next time you open it.

How to install

    How it will look on your phone

    BRIANE Appears on your home screen with this icon, in your app list, and in your recent apps — just like a normal installed app.

    Installing does not change your account, your phone number, or the alerts you receive. You can remove it any time by deleting the icon from your home screen.
